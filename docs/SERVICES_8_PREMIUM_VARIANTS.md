# Services Premium Design Family

Eight compatible Services designs are now available:

1. services_bento_premium — Services Bento Premium
2. services_editorial_premium — Services Editorial Premium
3. services_showcase_premium — Services Showcase Premium
4. services_minimal_luxury — Services Minimal Luxury
5. services_contrast_premium — Services Contrast Premium
6. services_split_premium — Services Split Premium
7. services_grid_premium — Services Grid Premium
8. services_feature_premium — Services Feature Premium

## Luna behavior

Relative redesign requests on a selected Services section, such as:
- Make this section feel more premium.
- Make this section more modern.
- Make this section better.
- Make it more visually interesting.

prefer a DIFFERENT compatible Services design and preserve the section purpose,
useful copy, service meaning, CTA intent, current website theme, and industry context.

The seven new designs use the same counted service field contract as Bento Premium
(up to seven services), so add/remove service CRUD remains compatible across the family.

## Design directions

- Bento: asymmetrical modular card composition
- Editorial: asymmetric magazine-style list
- Showcase: large image-led feature plus cards
- Minimal Luxury: whitespace-heavy restrained typography
- Contrast Premium: higher-contrast composition that follows the active theme
- Split Premium: alternating split service rows
- Grid Premium: refined three-column cards
- Feature Premium: one dominant service plus supporting cards
